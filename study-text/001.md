## Slide 2

INTRODUCTION
Angina is a type of chest pain caused by reduced blood flow to the heart. Angina is a symptom of coronary artery disease.
Angina is also called angina pectoris. It is often described as squeezing, pressure heaviness, tightness or pain in the chest. It may feel like heavy weight laying on the chest.
The pain with angina can be new or recurring pain that goes away with treatment.

## Slide 3

SYMPTOMS
The angina symptoms include chest pain and discomfort. The chest pain or discomfort may feel like:
Heart burn
Abdominal fullness
Pressure on the chest
Squeezing
Pain
Dizziness
Fatigue, dyspnoea, nausea, and sweating.

## Slide 5

TYPES
STABLE ANGINA: Stable angina is the most common form of angina. It usually happens during activity, also called exertion. It goes away with rest or medication.
UNSTABLE ANGINA: This type angina requires immediate medical intervention. Unstable angina is unpredictable and occurs at rest. Or the pain is worsening and occurs with less physical activity. Unstable angina can last up to 20 minutes unlike stable angina which last for 5 minutes.
VARIANT ANGINA(PRINZMETAL ANGINA): This type of angina is not caused by CAD. It is usually caused by spasms in the heart arteries. This spasm temporarily reduces blood flow to the heart.
