## Slide 2

DEFINITION
Congestive heart failure occurs when the heart is unable to pump blood throughout the body efficiently.
Early diagnosis, lifestyle changes, and medical treatment can help manage CHF and prevent it from progressing.

## Slide 3

CAUSES
High blood pressure (hypertension)
Coronary artery disease
Valve conditions
Diabetes
Thyroid disease
obesity

## Slide 5

MEDICAL MANAGEMENT
There are several medications that can be used to treat CHF, including ACE inhibitors, beta blockers, etc...
ACE inhibitors: lisinopril, enalapril, ramipril 5-10mg OD,
POTASIUM SPARING DIURETICS eg spironolactone 25-100mg OD, Eplerenone,
NONSTEROIDAL ANTI-INFLAMMATORY DRUGS (NSAIDs): such as ibuprofen 400mg tds, aspirin 300mg tds, naproxen 50-100mg tds
BETA-BLOCKERS: Atenolol 50-100mg OD, Bisoprolol 2.5-5mg OD, Metoprolol 50-100mg OD.

## Slide 6

MEDICAL MANAGEMENT CONTS...
LOOP DIURETICS: Furosemide 40mg tds, Torsemide
THIAZIDE DIURETICS: This cause the blood vessels to widen and help the body remove any extra fluid. Eg hydrochlothiazide 25-50mg OD, Indapamide 5-10mg OD.
NITRATES eg isorsobide dinitrates,
SURGERIES AND PROCEDURES: eg Angioplasty, a procedure to open blocked arteries.
VALVE REPAIR SURGERY: To help the valves open and close properly.
