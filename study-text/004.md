## Slide 2

DEFINITION
Coronary heart disease is a type of heart disease where the arteries of the heart cannot deliver enough oxygen-rich blood to the heart.
Imbalance between myocardial oxygen supply and demand.
It is also sometimes called coronary artery disease or ischaemic heart disease.

SIGNS AND SYMPTOMS
Sometimes coronary heart disease is silent. Meaning one may not have any symptoms until the individual experience heart attack or other complications. This can make coronary heart disease difficult to diagnose before a problem occurs. The symptoms of heart attack include the following:
Angina
Cold sweats
Dizziness
Light headedness
Shortness of breath, nausea, neck pain, fatique,

## Slide 6

MEDICAL MANAGEMENT
The treatment of coronary heart disease depends on how serious the symptoms are and any other health conditions attached.
Some medicines can reduce or prevent chest pain and manage other medical conditions that may contribute to coronary heart disease.
As part of the treatment, medicines that widen the blood vessels and help the heart beat with less force are prescribe. These family of medicines for such treatment are: ACE inhibitors, Beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, nitrates, statins such as atorvastatin, rosuvastatin,
Also antiplatelet therapy e.g, Low dose of Aspirin 75mg, and clopidogrel 75mg daily.

## Slide 8

MEDICAL MANAGEMENT CONTS....
The patient with coronary heart disease may need a procedure or heart surgery.
PERCUTENEOUS CORONARY INTERVENTION(PCI) This procedure involve opening coronary arteries that are narrowed or blocked by the build up of plaque. This procedure is done through the use of shock waves to break up the hardened plaque and may also implant a small mesh tube, or stent, in the artery to prevent it from narrowing again.

## Slide 9

MEDICAL MANAGEMENT CONTS...
Coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG): This improves blood flow to the heart by using the healthy arteries from the chest wall and veins from the legs to bypass the blocked arteries.
BARIATRIC SURGERY: This help lower the risk of coronary artery disease and other problems that affects the blood vessels for people with obesity, and also for people who has diabetes.

## Slide 10

NURSING MANAGEMENT
Monitor patient vital signs
Alleviate patient anxiety and reassure family members
Conduct general physical examination to rule out other abnormalities such as edema
Listen or auscultate the lungs for crackle sounds
Make the patient comfortable
-Weigh the patient daily to ensure that they are not fluid overload
-Administer oxygen as prescribe
-Administer medications as prescribe
