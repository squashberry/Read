## Slide 2

DEFINITION
Valvular heart disease occurs when one of the four valves in the heart fails to operate as usual.

## Slide 3

TYPES
There are three types of valvular heart disease:
1. REGURGITATION: This occurs when the heart valve fails to close tightly, leading to backflow of blood.
2. STENOSIS :This is when the heart valves are too small making it hard for blood to pass through the heart and force the heart to work harder to pump the blood.
3. ATRESIA: This means that a heart valve has no opening at all. Instead, a solid tissue forms that blocks blood flow. It is usually present at birth but can rarely develop later in life. The two main types are pulmonary and tricuspid atresia.

## Slide 7

MANAGEMENT
Heart valve disease treatments depends on the cause and may include:
Protecting the valve from further damage
Taking medications
Surgery or invasive procedures if necessary
MEDICATIONS
Although, medicines cannot cure heart valve disease, they and healthy lifestyle can help with symptoms and decrease your chances of having a stroke or cardiac arrest.
